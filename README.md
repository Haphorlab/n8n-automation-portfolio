## 🚀 Projects

### [Automated Content Discovery & Publishing System](./automated-content-discovery-system)

AI-powered content pipeline that discovers, scores, summarizes, and publishes articles automatically. Handles 100+ articles daily with GPT-4 powered filtering and summarization.

**Key Features:**
* AI relevance scoring (0-100 scale)
* Automated deduplication
* Human-sounding content summarization
* SEO optimization (meta descriptions, tags)
* WordPress-ready output
* Multi-phase pipeline (discovery → publishing)

**Tech Stack:** n8n, OpenAI GPT-4, Airtable, RSS  
**Use Case:** Commercial real estate firms, content marketing teams, news aggregators  
**Business Impact:** Eliminates 20+ hours/week of manual content curation
